# IELTS Writing Band 8+ Sample Collection

Sc## ope

This catalog separates official examiner-marked material from supplementary model-answer pages. A page calling an essay “Band 8” is not, by itself, proof that it was assessed by an official IELTS examiner.

## Official examiner-marked corpus

The official IELTS PDFs in this directory contain candidate scripts, reported band scores and examiner comments. The current official sample set is useful for learning the marking process, but its published scripts are not exclusively Band 8.0 or higher. Do not relabel those scores as Band 8+.

- [Academic official sample responses and examiner comments](academic-writing-sample-responses-examiner-comments.md)
- [General Training official sample responses and examiner comments](general-training-writing-sample-responses-examiner-comments.md)
- [Official public band descriptors](../official/ielts-writing-band-descriptors.md)

## Supplementary Band 8+ model-answer sources

For local lab demonstrations, the repository also contains an explicitly synthetic set:

- [Synthetic Band 8+ essays and rubric simulations](synthetic-band-8-plus-essays.md)

It is suitable for retrieval tests, including Cause and Effect, Opinion and Discussion essay queries. It must be cited as `source_kind=synthetic`, never as an official examiner record.

These are useful discovery links, but they are not presented as official examiner reports. Keep their attribution and score claim when adding a page to the corpus.

## Acceptance rule for future additions

Add a sample as `official_examiner_comment` only when the source explicitly identifies IELTS/BC/IDP/Cambridge provenance and includes both a reported band score and examiner comment. Otherwise use `supplementary_model_answer` and preserve the original URL. This prevents the RAG system from answering that an unverified online essay received an official Band 8 score.
